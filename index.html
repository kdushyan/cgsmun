Letter from the Secretary-General

A Stage for the World's Future Voices

CGS Model United Nations Club

We founded this club on a simple conviction: that the most pressing questions of our time deserve to be debated by the generation who will inherit their consequences. Every resolution written, every motion raised, every compromise forged in our committees is practice for the diplomacy our world urgently needs.

Whether you step into our chambers for the first time or return as a veteran delegate, we promise you a forum that challenges your intellect, broadens your perspective, and connects you with a community of passionate young leaders. The stars, as we say, are reached through adversity—and we are here to help you reach them.
